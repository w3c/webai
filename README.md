

# Web & AI Interest Group (WebAI IG)

Welcome to the **[Web &amp; AI Interest Group](https://www.w3.org/groups/ig/webai/) (WebAI IG)** repository!

We are delighted to have you join us. The mission of the Web & AI Interest Group is to provide a forum to explore and discuss how emerging Artificial Intelligence (AI) related technologies are impacting and intersecting with Web technologies and consider how the community could collaborate to shape these evolutions for the benefit of web users.

Everyone who participates in the WebAI IG is expected to behave in accordance with the [W3C Code of Conduct](https://www.w3.org/policies/code-of-conduct/).

## How to Get Involved & Contribute

This GitHub repository is a central hub for our collaborative work. We welcome your input and participation.

### Have a new idea, concern, or proposal related to AI & the Web?

**Please [open a new Issue](https://github.com/w3c/webai/issues)** to start a discussion in this repository.
This can include:

- New use cases or requirements
- Potential risks or opportunities
- Architectural suggestions
- Feedback on existing work
- Pointers to new industry developments

We encourage early discussion — even rough ideas are welcome.

### Contributing

You can contribute by:

- Participating in discussions on GitHub issues
- Joining meetings and presenting ideas or prototypes
- Helping draft IG Notes, use cases, and analyses
- Providing insights from industry, academia, or standards work

The IG’s process is **open** and **collaborative**, and contributions from all backgrounds are welcome. As work items and deliverables are scoped, we will create dedicated branches, [project](https://github.com/orgs/w3c/projects/218/views/1) or repositories for collaborative discussion. 



## Upcoming Work

### Potential Deliverables

The group explores various topics that may lead to future standardization work or informative reports. Our current charter outlines several potential deliverables, including (but not limited to):

* **[Ethical Principles for Web Machine Learning](https://www.w3.org/TR/webmachinelearning-ethics/)** (joint deliverable with the Web Machine Learning Working Group)
* **[The Agentic Web: risks and opportunities](https://github.com/w3c/ai-web-impact/blob/main/agent-report.md)**
* **[AI &amp; the Web: Understanding and managing the impact of Machine Learning models on the Web](https://www.w3.org/reports/ai-web-impact/)**
* **[Threat Model for Agentic AI Web Browsers](https://github.com/w3c-cg/threat-modeling/blob/main/models/agentic-ai-web-browsers.md)** (joint deliverable with the Threat Modeling Community Group)
* **[Threat Model for AI in the Browser](https://github.com/w3c-cg/threat-modeling/blob/main/models/ai-in-browser.md)** (joint deliverable with the Threat Modeling Community Group)

### Workshop

We will coordinate on the [AI Agents &amp; Web Browsers workshop](https://github.com/w3c/strategy/issues/507).
